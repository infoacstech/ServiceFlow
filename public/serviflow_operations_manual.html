1. Executive Summary & Product Architecture

ServiFlow is a cloud-native, multi-tenant Field Service Operations and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform designed specifically for service contractors, installation specialists, and engineering agencies (including CCTV, Solar, HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, and Equipment Repair).

Core Infrastructure

Built with React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Google Cloud Firestore, and PWA/TWA Service Worker caching for instant offline availability.

Live Cloud Sync

Continuous OTA (Over-The-Air) deployment via Firebase Hosting (serviceflow-969f5.web.app) allowing real-time feature updates without repeated app-store submissions.

3. Step-by-Step Operational Workflow

Step 3.1: Creating Customers & Registering Assets

  • Navigate to Customers tab > click Add Customer.
  • Enter Name, Mobile Number (with WhatsApp capability), full Address, and GPS Landmark.
  • Under Customer Assets, record equipment details (e.g. "Daikin 1.5T Inverter AC - Outdoor S/N 84729").
  • Bulk import existing lists using the built-in CSV template (serviflow_customer_sample.csv).

Step 3.2: Preparing Estimates & Quotations

  • Open Quotations tab > click Create Quotation.
  • Select the customer; line items will automatically calculate Rate × Qty with applicable GST slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%).
  • Click Share via WhatsApp to generate a formatted message or click Print / PDF to render a clean A4 quote.
  • Once the client approves, click Convert to Invoice or Convert to Job in 1 click.

Step 3.3: Dispatching Service Jobs to Technicians

  • Open Jobs tab > click New Job.
  • Select Service Category, Customer, Priority (Emergency, High, Medium, Low), and assign a Field Technician.
  • The assigned technician instantly receives the job in their mobile portal.

Step 3.4: Field Execution (Technician Mobile View)

  • Technician logs in > opens Technician Portal.
  • Taps Navigate (Google Maps) to reach the client address.
  • Taps Check In (GPS Punch) to verify on-site arrival.
  • Takes Job Proof Photos (before/after), records parts used from truck inventory, and captures the client's Digital Signature.
  • Marks job as Completed; an automated WhatsApp completion summary is prepared for the customer.

Step 3.5: Billing, Invoicing & UPI Payments

  • Open Invoices tab > view balance due amounts.
  • Click Record Payment: enter amount received (supports Partial or Full), choose method (UPI, Cash, Bank Transfer, Cheque), and input transaction UTR number.
  • Customer balance is instantly updated with real-time zero-clamping safeguards.

4. Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Management

ServiFlow contains an automated recurring service engine for multi-year contracts:

  • Contract Creation: Set Start Date, Expiry Date, and Visit Frequency (Monthly = 12 visits, Quarterly = 4 visits, Bi-Annual = 2 visits).
  • Automated Reminder Engine: In the AMC tab, upcoming visits are highlighted as Due Today, Due in 7 Days, or Overdue.
  • Single-Click Dispatch: Tap "Schedule Visit" to spawn an operational job card for your field engineer without re-typing customer address or equipment specs.
  • WhatsApp Renewal Notices: Send pre-configured renewal quotation links 30 days prior to contract expiration.

7. Emergency Operations & Disaster Recovery

  1. How to issue updates without re-uploading to Play Store?
    Make your changes in AI Studio. When the build succeeds, Firebase Hosting automatically updates. When users next open the app on Android, the local service worker fetches the updated files automatically.
  2. Offline Operation for Field Engineers:
    If a technician has no cellular network on an underground site, they can still view their active job cards, capture notes, and complete checklist steps. Data is safely cached in browser IndexedDB and syncs to Firestore the moment connection returns.
  3. Account Deletion Request (Play Store Compliance):
    If a business owner requests data deletion, navigate to Settings > System Maintenance and execute operational reset, or process via email request to uniquesolutions108@gmail.com within 48 hours.
