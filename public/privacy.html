ServiFlow — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Product of Expert Technologies • Google Play Compliant

1. Overview

ServiFlow is an enterprise field operations and service workforce management software developed by Expert Technologies. We provide tools for field service businesses (CCTV, Solar, HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, and Equipment Repair) to dispatch technicians, manage recurring AMC contracts, issue invoices, and record job completion proofs.

2. Information Collection & Device Permissions

To provide core mobile workforce and service tracking functionality, ServiFlow requests specific device permissions:

Location Data (GPS)

Captured during technician check-in, attendance geofence verification, and work order completion. Location is only accessed during active user interaction and is not tracked continuously when the app is closed.

Camera & Images

Used by technicians on site to capture installation proof photos, equipment serial barcodes, and customer digital signatures on service sheets.

Microphone / Audio

Optional speech-to-text recording allowing technicians to dictate job notes and diagnostic details hands-free while servicing hardware.

Customer & Contact Records

Customer contact names, phone numbers, and job site addresses entered by users are strictly used for dispatching technicians, issuing estimates, and sending billing notices.

3. Data Protection & Security

All data is transmitted via secure HTTPS (TLS 1.3 encryption) and stored in Google Cloud Firestore with strict multi-tenant isolation. No cross-tenant access is permitted.

4. Third-Party Services & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, license, or monetize any user or customer data to third-party data brokers or marketing firms. We only rely on trusted cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform) and WhatsApp Web deep links to facilitate client communication.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

Google Play Account Deletion Compliance:

You have full authority to request deletion of your account and all stored business records. You can initiate a deletion directly in the app via Settings > System Maintenance > Reset Data or by emailing uniquesolutions108@gmail.com. Records will be permanently deleted within 48 hours.
